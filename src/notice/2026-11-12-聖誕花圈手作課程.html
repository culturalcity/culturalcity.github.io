管委會邀請花藝老師開設聖誕花圈手作課程，以乾燥花材製作直徑約 27～30 公分的花圈，完成後可掛在門上或擺在室內。歡迎住戶報名參加。

課程資訊
時間2026年12月19日（六）14:00 起，約一個半至兩小時
地點1F 交誼廳或 2F 健身房（視報名人數而定，另行通知）
費用花材費每份 300 元（講師費由管委會負擔）
名額10 位，報名踴躍將視情況加開
截止2026年12月4日（五）18:00
報名方式

請填寫線上報名表單，填入戶號樓層與聯絡人姓名。

前往報名表單
注意事項
  • 一份一人報名：一份花材可由兩人共同製作，請只報名一位。
  • 開課門檻：截止時未滿 5 人，課程取消。
  • 繳費確認：截止後以 LINE 發送預約完成通知，請於收到後三日內至 1F 大廳向總幹事繳交花材費 300 元，完成後才算確認名額；可開立收費憑證。花材費由老師用於採購花材。
  • 如有疑問請洽管理中心。

The Management Committee has invited a floral instructor to lead a Christmas wreath workshop. Using dried flowers, each participant will make a wreath about 27–30 cm in diameter, to hang on your door or display indoors. All residents are welcome to sign up.

Workshop Details
Date & TimeSaturday, 19 December 2026, from 14:00 (about 1.5–2 hours)
Venue1F Lounge or 2F Gym, depending on the number of participants (to be confirmed)
FeeNT$300 per set of flower materials (the instructor's fee is covered by the Management Committee)
Places10; more may be added if demand is high
DeadlineFriday, 4 December 2026, 18:00
How to Register

Please fill in the online form with your unit number, floor and a contact name.

Open Registration Form
Notes
  • One registration per set: Two people may work on one set together; please register only one person.
  • Minimum numbers: The workshop will be cancelled if fewer than 5 people have registered by the deadline.
  • Payment: After the deadline, a booking confirmation will be sent via LINE. Please pay the NT$300 materials fee to the Property Manager in the 1F Lobby within three days of receiving it; your place is confirmed once paid. A receipt can be issued. The fee goes to the instructor to purchase the materials.
  • For questions, please contact the Management Center.
