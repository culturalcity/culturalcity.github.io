說明

地下停車場 B1F 39 號車位旁柱面長期受潮，粉刷層已出現劣化剝落。經第五屆第三次管理委員會議決議，委由信盛工程先行剔除已劣化的粉刷層，觀察柱體是否有滲漏水點，再據以決定後續修復工法。

  • 施工期間請避免進出 B1F 停車區域。

後續處理

本次為第一階段的剔除檢查。剔除後如發現滲漏水點，將另行評估止漏工法；如無滲漏，則進行重新粉刷。後續施工將另行公告。

Background

The plaster on the column beside Parking Space No. 39 on B1F of the Basement Parking Lot has deteriorated and is flaking after prolonged exposure to damp. Following the resolution of the 3rd meeting of the 5th Management Committee, Trust Same Construction has been commissioned to first remove the deteriorated plaster and inspect the column for water seepage points, so that the appropriate repair method can then be determined.

What Residents Are Asked to Do
  • Please avoid entering or leaving the B1F parking area during the work.
  • Dust may drift to nearby parking spaces. If your vehicle is parked near the work area on B1F, you may move it for the day or cover it with a dust sheet.
What Happens Next

This is the first stage — removal and inspection only. If water seepage is found, a waterproofing method will be assessed separately; if none is found, the column will be re-plastered. Any further work will be announced separately.

We apologize for any inconvenience. Work is carried out by Trust Same Construction. For any questions, please contact the Management Center.
